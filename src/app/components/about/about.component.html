About me

Web development that
moves the business

I'm Guillermo Mateos Santos, a full-stack developer based in Seville. I build applications with Angular, Next.js and Laravel, and automate business processes with Python and n8n: system integrations, large-scale scraping and data migrations between CRMs/ERPs such as HubSpot and Odoo.

I work with an AI-assisted, agentic workflow (Claude Code as my daily driver, MCP servers, local LLMs with Ollama), which lets me ship from idea to production in weeks instead of months — without giving up on code quality or security.

I don't just write code that works — I build digital products with real impact. I've worked at real companies (CodeArts SL, Guadaltech), migrated the CRM of an agri-food business, extracted and normalised a 17,517-product catalogue for an Odoo migration, and shipped a multi-tenant SaaS that is live in production.

I'm relocating to Gdańsk in October 2026 and I'm open to roles in the Tri-City area — on-site, hybrid or remote. English B2 (Cambridge), Spanish native.
